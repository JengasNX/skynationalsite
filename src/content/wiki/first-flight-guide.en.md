Plan your first online flight without rushing: block out two or three hours to walk calmly through every stage, from terminal to terminal.

## Choosing the route

- A leg of **up to 500 NM**: long enough to feel every phase of flight, short enough not to get tired.
- A departure airport with a simple taxi layout and a runway you can find without stress.
- An arrival airport with a published approach (ILS or RNAV) — it is far easier than navigating point by point to the very edge.
- Fields where controllers are on duty per the community schedule: fly your first leg under their control.
- A route that actually exists in the network, not a hypothetical one — check what really flies between those airports.

## Planning and pre-flight documents

1. Build the route in a flight planner (SimBrief and similar) along the great circle between the two airports.
2. Pick a SID at the departure airport and a STAR at the arrival airport and connect them to the main track.
3. Choose a flight level: the magnetic course decides which series of levels applies, and the distance sets the practical ceiling.
4. Calculate the fuel: trip, taxi, contingency, alternate, reserve.
5. Save the document set: flight plan, METAR and TAF for both airports, NOTAMs, approach charts.

## Start, taxi and takeoff

- Before connecting: simulator running, aircraft on the stand, mass and CG set.
- Connect to the network, switch on COM1 and request your IFR clearance from delivery, if there is one.
- From ground control you will receive a taxi route: read it back and taxi as instructed, staying on the right side of the centrelines.
- At the holding point wait for the instruction: "line up and wait" means occupy the runway and hold, "cleared for takeoff" means roll.
- Once the vertical speed is positive, raise the gear and climb at the planned speed.

## Climb, level cruise and the centre frequency

Get your climb clearance, switch to the departure frequency as instructed and report your altitude and intentions briefly. Level off at the assigned flight level, keep the track and wait for the handoff to the centre frequency — the controller usually initiates it with "contact center".

## Descent, landing and parking

The descent starts early: you will receive "descend and maintain", and altitude and speed restrictions at the waypoints must be honoured. On approach expect vectors or a procedural clearance; after touchdown vacate the runway, report it, contact ground and taxi to the stand. Shut the engines down on the park and disconnect from the network.

## Newcomer checklist

- Route and flight level written down in front of you.
- Frequencies numbered in the order they will be used.
- Taxi and approach charts open in a separate window.
- Weather and NOTAMs re-read one hour before departure.
- Phones, chats and extra programs closed for the duration of the flight.
