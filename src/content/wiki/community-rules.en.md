This policy describes how we live and work together. It is binding for every member of SkyNational Community — pilots, controllers and guests of the Discord server.

## Respect and tolerance

- Keep the conversation on topic and never make it personal: criticism addresses actions, not people.
- Newcomer mistakes are a reason to explain, not to mock. Online flying is learned mostly from other people's transmissions.
- Requests, conflicts and arguments are settled in the designated channels, never mid-flight and never on a live frequency.
- Toxicity, trolling and provocation are a direct violation and grounds for a ban.

## Honesty on the network

- **No fake callsigns.** One person — one account, one callsign, one connection.
- Never use someone else's callsign, including callsigns of aircraft belonging to other members.
- Do not earn an AVOID flag: no reason to be avoided means no flag.
- Flight data (aircraft type, route, flight level) must match what is actually being flown.

## Following VATSIM rules

We work inside the network, not on top of it: VATSIM rules, controller instructions and standard procedure take priority over any member's preferences. Not knowing the network code is no excuse — read the official network documents before your first flight.

## Confidentiality

- **Discord IDs, phone numbers, email addresses and other personal data must not be posted** in public channels or on the website.
- Private correspondence is never forwarded to third parties or shared as screenshots.
- Photos, flight recordings and member material are published only with their owner's consent.

## Roles: CUR, SUP and SCD

- **CUR** — curator: runs the ADM/MODR area, helps newcomers, looks into disputed situations and keeps order in the channel.
- **SUP** — support: handles tickets, answers questions, receives reports and passes them on according to the policy.
- **SCD** — moderator: enforces the rules in real time, issues warnings and suspends access when violations occur.

## Sanctions and how to file a report

Sanctions are applied progressively: **warning → second warning → temporary ban → permanent ban**. Serious violations — fake callsigns, insults, leaking personal data — may lead to an immediate ban.

A report is filed as a support ticket: name the member, give the date and time, describe the violation and attach evidence (screenshots, chat records). We do not settle disputes in public channels, and the answer comes in the same ticket.
