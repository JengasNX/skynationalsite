On September 21, SkyNational held its **first group flight** — and it went well. Crews from several countries gathered on the apron at Sheremetyevo (UUEE) to fly together to St. Petersburg.

## How It Went

The ramp-up went exactly as planned: a joint briefing, an assigned departure sequence, and a comms check. On the runway each aircraft held its own moment, and the trail formation came together easily since the route had been laid out in advance.

The climb was flown on shared flight levels: pilots kept a common profile and reported their position regularly. Approaching ULLI, the controllers sequenced the traffic into a smooth rotation, and the landing was almost by the textbook — even spacing and a calm glide path all the way down.

## What the Flight Gave Us

Even a short sector from Sheremetyevo to Pulkovo gave the crews a lot to take away: preparing for an hour-long flight, climbing and cruising, an instrument descent, and the final phase in evening traffic. For many, this was their first experience launching in a trail and their first flight under network procedures — and everyone handled it.

The ground side deserves special mention too: pilots starting later studied the aircraft ahead of them in advance to avoid conflicting situations on the runway. That order kept the departures calm and predictable for everyone involved.

## Thanks

Thank you to everyone who took to the air that evening. Special thanks to those who helped newcomers on the ground and in the air — that is exactly how a community should work. We would also like to acknowledge the crews who handled the radio work so smoothly.

## What Comes Next

This was the first flight, but certainly not the last. The schedule for the next group departures is already taking shape, with shorter routes for beginners as well as longer sectors. Watch the Events section on the website and the announcements in Discord.
