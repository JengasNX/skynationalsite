We have launched the **wiki section** — SkyNational's knowledge base. It is the learning part of the website, collecting everything a pilot needs online, from the very first steps to handling busy traffic.

## Categories

To start with, the wiki is organized into five areas:

- **Getting Started.** VATSIM registration, obtaining your pilot ID, and installing the network and your first client.
- **Flying Online.** Preparing for your first flight: route planning, calculated performance, and flight level selection.
- **Regulations.** The community's core rules and the general conduct expected on the network.
- **Hardware and Software.** A look at popular simulators, flight planners, and supporting tools.
- **Air Traffic Control.** For those who want to control: the fundamentals, standard procedures, and useful material.

## What Is Inside

The articles are written in plain language and focus on practice. You will find step-by-step registration guides, a walkthrough of your first supervised flight, basic radio phraseology, and software setup tips.

## This Is Just the Beginning

The wiki is a living section. We will keep expanding it as the community grows — new articles will follow group flights and training sessions. If you feel a topic is missing, let us know in Discord and we will add it to the plan.

Articles are written by community members with flying experience, and each one is reviewed before publication so that the advice stays current. Every material follows the same structure: a short theory block, a step-by-step part, and answers to the questions newcomers ask most often. That format makes it easy to find a quick answer without wading through long texts.

Anyone willing to contribute is welcome — writing an article is a good way to consolidate your own knowledge and help those coming after you. Proposed topics are coordinated in Discord, and we are always happy to help with the formatting.
