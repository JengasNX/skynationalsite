Autumn is flying season at SkyNational. We have put together a **schedule of upcoming events** so you can plan your participation in advance.

## First Flights Week — October 25

The month's main event for newcomers. Over the course of a week we will hold a series of online sessions covering VATSIM registration, client installation, flight planning, and your first flight with experienced pilots alongside you. Details and the schedule are in the Events section.

## Get-Togethers

Regular online meetups where you can get to know the members, ask questions, and hear about the community's plans. The format is an easy-going conversation in a Discord voice channel, and no preparation is required. Both long-time members and people who have just joined show up, so you can join the conversation at any experience level and ask a question at any point during the discussion.

## Night Group Flight: UUEE to EHAM — November 14

The largest event of the season: a night flight from Sheremetyevo to Amsterdam on board an A320neo. Briefing and departure in the evening UTC, flown under VATSIM rules. It is a great chance to practice a night profile, an approach after a long cruise, and flying in busy European airspace.

## How to Stay Informed

The full schedule is published in the **Events** section of the website, with announcements mirrored in Discord. Registration for each event opens in advance — we recommend signing up early so we can plan the briefing and the departure sequence.

Participation in every activity is free, and no preparation is needed: newcomers are helped on the spot, while experienced pilots get a chance to sharpen their skills in friendly company. If you are unsure whether the format suits you, just come and take a look — that is how almost every member of the community started. After each event we publish a short report, so even those who could not make it into the air stay in the loop.
