We are glad to announce that **SkyNational Community is open**. This is an international aviation community created for VATSIM pilots, aviation enthusiasts, and everyone who dreams of the sky. We bring together people from every corner of the world — from beginner virtual pilots to experienced captains who know how to handle an aircraft when things get demanding.

## What We Have Planned

We are only getting started, but the roadmap is already clear:

- **Group flights.** Regular shared departures on routes published in advance, from short domestic sectors to long-haul trips.
- **Events and meetups.** Online get-togethers, themed evenings, and community-wide celebrations.
- **A wiki knowledge base.** A learning section with material for both newcomers and seasoned pilots — from VATSIM registration to the finer points of radio communication.
- **Team recruitment.** We are looking for like-minded people in management and support roles to grow the community together.

## The Principles Behind the Community

SkyNational rests on three simple principles: **mutual support**, **respect**, and **safety**. Experienced pilots help newcomers make sense of flight planning and radio work, while those still learning the simulator can always ask a question without fearing a dismissive laugh. We are deliberately aiming for a calm atmosphere: making mistakes at the start is normal — what matters is learning from them and moving on.

It is also worth highlighting the international character of the project. Routes of different lengths and across different time zones are planned, so pilots from anywhere in the world can take part at a time that suits them. The community communicates in both Russian and English.

## Join Us

SkyNational grew out of a simple idea: flying online is a lot more rewarding when you are not alone. Whether you are just starting out or have already logged hundreds of hours, there is something here for you to try and experience to share.

Joining is easy: visit our Discord server, get to know the members, and pick the direction that interests you — flying, training, or helping the project grow. There are no obligations at the start; you are welcome to simply come and see how everything works.

Watch for updates on the website and in Discord: announcements of our first events and details of the team recruitment are coming soon. Welcome on board.
