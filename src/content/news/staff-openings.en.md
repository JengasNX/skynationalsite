The community is growing, and we need people. We are opening **recruitment for the SkyNational team** and inviting everyone who wants to shape the life of the community — not just fly in it — to join.

## Open Roles

We are currently looking for candidates for the following positions:

- **CFO** — Chief Financial Officer: managing the community's finances.
- **CCO** — Chief Commercial Officer: partnerships and commercial directions.
- **CGO** — Chief Growth Officer: development and bringing in new members.
- **CSO** — Chief Security Officer: security and order on the network.
- **CHO** — Chief Human Officer: working with people and the team.
- **CDO** — Chief Development Officer: projects and infrastructure growth.
- **CUR** — Curator ADM/MODR: curating the administration and moderation teams.
- **SUP** — Support-Ticket: handling member requests.
- **SCD** — Moderator: moderating chats and events.

## What We Expect and What We Offer

All community roles are volunteer positions. We expect regular participation, a responsible attitude to your duties, and a willingness to work as part of a team. In return, you get real influence over how the project develops, hands-on experience in organizing aviation events, and a circle of like-minded people from around the world.

Please note: for most roles you do not need an accounting degree or prior experience in online administrations. What matters more is understanding why the role exists and a willingness to learn — we will help you pick up everything else together. Every new team member is paired with a mentor, so getting up to speed is straightforward.

## How to Apply

Applying is simple: fill in the **"Join Us"** form on the website, available in the main menu. Tell us about your experience and which role interests you most. We review every application and reach out to the candidates who fit.

Even if you are not sure you match the description but still want to contribute, go ahead and apply. For us, engagement and a willingness to deliver matter as much as prior experience.
