Everyone is invited to SkyNational's **night group flight**: Sheremetyevo (UUEE) to Amsterdam (EHAM). The start is in the evening UTC, and flying in darkness makes for a great chance to practice night operations and an approach after a long cruise.

## Program

- **18:30 UTC** — briefing: route, flight levels, departure sequence, and the specifics of the approach into EHAM.
- **19:00 UTC** — departure. Each aircraft launches on its assigned time slot.
- **A320neo** on board — or any other aircraft you prefer. What matters is matching flight levels and discipline in the air.

## Requirements

- A VATSIM account.
- An installed network client.
- Basic radio communication skills.

## How to Take Part

Details, the briefing pack, and participant sign-up are in the Discord event. Confirm your attendance in advance so we can add you to the plan and reserve your slot on departure. Questions about preparation can be asked in the community chat before the event.
