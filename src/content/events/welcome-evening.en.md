On September 27, SkyNational held its **first online welcome evening**. In a relaxed setting, members got to know each other and learned how the community is put together.

## What We Discussed

- **Community presentation.** The idea, the goals, and the principles behind SkyNational.
- **Structure and roles.** How the management works, what each role is responsible for, and how volunteers take part in the life of the project.
- **Plans for the near future.** Group flights, the training week, and the launch of the wiki knowledge base.

## Member Q&A

A large part of the evening went to questions: how the team recruitment works, whether there will be training for newcomers, and what event formats are planned. Every question was answered in detail, and the most interesting topics were added to the agenda of future meetings.

## Outcome

The evening confirmed one thing: interest in flying together is real, and it goes both ways. Thank you to everyone who joined. Further get-togethers will become part of the regular schedule — announcements will appear in the Events section.
