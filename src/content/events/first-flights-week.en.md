**First Flights Week** is SkyNational's series of online sessions for those just starting out on the network. The format is hands-on workshops with time for questions, and participation is free of charge.

## What We Cover

- VATSIM registration and getting your pilot ID.
- Installing and configuring your network client.
- Flight planning: route, calculated performance, and flight level selection.
- Your first flight under the guidance of experienced community pilots.

## Schedule

The sessions run throughout the week starting on October 25. The exact timetable is posted in Discord and in the Events section — pick the slots that suit you and drop in. Recordings of every session stay available, so you can catch up on anything you missed.

## How to Prepare

Bring your own simulator: any popular platform works. If something is not installed yet, do not worry — we will help you set it up during the first session. By the end of the week, every participant receives recommendations on how to keep practicing.
